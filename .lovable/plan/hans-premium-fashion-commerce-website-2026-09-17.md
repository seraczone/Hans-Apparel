# HANS premium fashion commerce website

## Overview
Build a complete, responsive editorial e-commerce experience for HANS, using the supplied official logo unchanged and a warm ivory, espresso, terracotta, sand, and restrained champagne design system. The site will prioritize shop discovery, WhatsApp ordering, bespoke enquiries, and premium African fashion storytelling.

## Pages and navigation
- Build the homepage in the exact requested section order, from announcement bar through footer.
- Add dedicated Shop, product detail, Collections, About, Bespoke, and Journal pages.
- Add shared desktop and mobile navigation, search, wishlist, cart, mobile menu, quick view, filters, and floating WhatsApp access.
- Keep all linked destinations real and navigable.

## Homepage experience
- Create the signature campaign hero with editorial copy on the left and a large African fashion model layered in front of a sculptural terracotta/sand arch on the right.
- Retain the arch and layered model composition on mobile, repositioned below the copy and actions.
- Build the feature strip, trust panel, second architectural editorial banner, HANS story, asymmetric category grid, product sections, featured campaign, craftsmanship story, campaign media, bespoke section, testimonials, Instagram grid, final call-to-action, and footer.
- Use restrained layered reveals, image motion, carousel controls, and reduced-motion fallbacks.

## Commerce and content structure
- Keep products, collections, testimonials, journal posts, categories, campaign copy, and brand settings in typed data files for straightforward future CMS migration.
- Support product filtering, sorting, wishlist state, cart quantities, quick view, galleries, size selection, and related products.
- Generate WhatsApp messages from one configurable brand setting, including product name, selected size, and current product URL.
- Use clearly labeled sample product names, prices, testimonials, and editorial entries that are easy to replace; no unsupported claims or statistics.

## Visual assets
- Preserve and use the uploaded HANS logo exactly, without redrawing, stretching, recoloring, or changing its proportions.
- Generate a cohesive set of premium African fashion campaign and product imagery with warm, cinematic, Abuja-inspired studio and architectural art direction.
- Use transparent-background model imagery for the two architectural compositions so the model visibly overlaps the shape rather than appearing in a rectangular photo.
- Optimize image loading and provide meaningful alt text.

## Design system and accessibility
- Define the supplied palette as semantic tokens and use Playfair Display with Inter at the requested hierarchy.
- Use sharp editorial controls, minimal shadows, restrained borders, generous whitespace, and varied full-width/asymmetric compositions.
- Provide keyboard operation, visible focus, named icon controls, semantic headings, accessible dialogs/drawers/forms, and sufficient contrast.
- Verify the principal layouts at desktop and mobile widths, including 360px and 414px.

## SEO and finishing
- Add unique metadata to every content page, canonical and Open Graph tags, Organization data on the homepage, Product and breadcrumb data on product pages, and descriptive URLs.
- Review desktop and mobile rendering, interactions, overflow, image layering, animation restraint, and the full conversion path.
- The WhatsApp number will remain a single clearly marked configuration value until the real business number is supplied.

## Technical notes
- Use the existing React, TypeScript, TanStack Start, Tailwind v4, shadcn, and Embla foundations.
- Keep shared shell elements and exactly one main content region in the root layout.
- No account backend, checkout payment processing, live inventory, CMS, or live Instagram feed will be added without a connected data service; the requested storefront interactions will work with structured sample data in this version.
