07D. MODEL LAYER
============================================================

Layer order:

↓

↓

↓
SUBTLE DECORATIVE ELEMENTS

The model should overlap the architectural shape.

Parts of the model/clothing may extend outside the shape.

This creates dimensionality.

The model should be large enough to visually anchor the entire
right side.

Do NOT make the model tiny.

Do NOT simply place a rectangular photo over the shape.

The model must feel integrated into the composition.
